The idea in one picture

Most ✨ columns read this line: your K/D against opponents from easy (left) to hard (right). It uses the ~200 players you fight most, not your whole career.

Why Honu and Fisu don’t show these

Why it matters: a player can show 3.96 lifetime K/D and only 1.15 against the people he really fights.

They look up one player. These columns also need the stats of your ~200 opponents, so this site keeps them all cached and refreshed. The 📊 Census / Fisu / Honu style columns are the same numbers the trackers show.

✨ Adjusted (the default columns)

⚔️ iVi — your overall rating

416
better than 59% of players

How good you are, once your opponents’ strength is counted.

  • High: you beat strong players at a good pace.
  • Low: your wins come from weak players, or you play slowly. Below zero shows as 0.
How it’s measured
  • Beating strong players counts more than beating weak ones.
  • Fast killing adds a bonus; slow, safe play costs a little.
  • Infantry only: vehicle and MAX kills above 10% lower it (🚜). Confirmed padders lose 10% (💩).
  • PlayStation: the 600 baseline is divided by the PC/PlayStation kill-speed gap (rechecked weekly), so top players compare fairly with PC.
  • PC: evened out for 🪳 Zerg: low 🪳 goes up a little, high 🪳 down, the average crowd (🪳 26.8) stays.

🎲 Luck

—

Wins more (+) or fewer (−) of the kills against the same opponents than ⚔️ iVi predicts. Not literally luck; vehicle kills are already adjusted.

A lasting positive luck means your ⚔️ iVi is understating you — you are growing faster than the rating. The rating catches up, and the luck settles back toward 0.

How it’s measured
  • Formula: for each opponent with 10+ kills between you, take your actual share of those kills minus the share the ⚔️ iVi model predicts from the two ratings — then average over all such opponents. +9% means you won 9 points more of the kills than the model expected.
  • Example: YuigahamaYui — gap −10‰ = −1% over 21 opponents (she beat 57% of them). About as expected, slightly unlucky.

Shrunk toward 0 for few opponents, so a player with only a handful of rivals never reads as wildly lucky or unlucky. The table marks it “a bit” past −48‰ / +64‰ and “much” past −71‰ / +150‰.

Assists per kill in recent Honu sessions: how often you help teammates get kills.

How it’s measured
  • Formula: assists ÷ kills in recent Honu sessions. An assist is a kill-assist XP tick (ids 2, 3, 371, 372) — you damaged the enemy and a teammate finished them.
  • Example: YuigahamaYui — 0.60, 60 assists per 100 kills, better than 79% of players.

Shown from 100 kills. Apr 1 – May 6, 2026 is skipped: a game bug recorded no assists then, so too few kills are left to measure. Higher means you share more kills with teammates — it feeds 🪳 Zerg, not ⚔️ iVi directly.

🪳 Zerg — riding a big group

79
better than 20% of players

The lower of two percentiles: 🎈 Inflation (your K/D lifted by easy fights) and 🤝 Assists (kills shared with teammates). 0–99. Shown from 100 kills in recent Honu sessions (“—” until then).

  • High: easy fights and lots of shared kills, at the same time. Lower is better.
  • Low: you earn your K/D in even fights, or you fight on your own.
How it’s measured

Why the lower of the two: a solo farmer of easy fights has high Inflation but few assists, and a team player in hard fights has many assists but no inflation. Neither is a zerg, so both stay low.

Crowd check: where we have Honu XP detail (50+ deaths, 2+ h), we count per death the revives, heals, shield repairs and ammo you received, plus engineer Hardlight barrier XP (it only ticks inside a big crowd). Zergs die in a crowd, so the crowd picks them up. Revives or the crowd score in the top quarter = Zerg confirmed (hover says so). Both in the bottom half = a high Zerg is softened step by step. Never raised. No 🪳 Zerg but a top-quarter crowd score = an estimate “~N”, shown only, never sorted or marked. No XP detail = no change.

Apr 1 – May 6, 2026 is skipped for 🤝 Assists and 🪳 Zerg: a game bug recorded no assists then (fixed May 6). Too few kills left = “—”.

🧼 Pure — your true solo ability, cleaned twice

234

What your rating would be if every fight counted as if you fought alone, and your K/D had to hold up against the fastest killers too. Pure iVi takes the crowd out; this takes the crowd and the cheese out.

How it’s measured
  • Formula: 🧘 Pure iVi × (1 − 🌈 G), where Pure iVi is adjs × (1 − 0.00436 × 🪳). The base is the Pure value — never the displayed (🪳-evened) ⚔️ iVi, or 🪳 gets charged twice.
  • Chosen, not measured: G is the share of cached players you beat on K/D and Brave at once — a rank share, not a cheating probability. Nothing in the data says 1% of G equals 1% of your score; that rate is a choice. The 🪳 half is measured (fitted on 915 duel pairs).
  • Example: Eyfia — Pure 921 at G 9.1% → 921 × (1 − 0.091) ≈ 838. Aekris — Pure 2667 at G 99.3% → 19.

Not a replacement for ⚔️ iVi. 🪳 and G are near-independent (r ≈ −0.06), so stacking them docks each sin once. PC only; no 🪳 score or no G → “—”.

🌈 G — how cheesy is this K/D?

21.44%

The share of all cached players you beat on K/D and Brave at once. A high K/D alone doesn't do it — you also need a collapsed 🦁 Brave. 🪖 Infantry doesn't score anymore: it only lives in the gate — the cheesy set is Brave under 1.7 and Infantry under 50%. The 1.7 is the trimmed mean 🦁 Brave of the top 100 by K/D on PC — the typical Brave of a top-K/D player (the raw mean, 6.68, is wrecked by two outliers at 461 and 41). On PlayStation the same figure is 2.9 (raw mean 3.17, top two at 19.24 and 13.05).

How it’s measured
  • Formula: (1 − K/D rank) × (1 − Brave rank), ranks over all cached players. Each factor is the share of players you beat on that one dimension.
  • Example: Tepe8756 — K/D 4.03 (rank 129 of 18,571), Brave 0.024 (rank 226): 0.9931 × 0.9878 ≈ 98.10%.

Most players sit at 10–23% (median 17.21%); 90% are under 28.95%. The top 100 start at 70.09%. — a different world from the rest.

🪖 Infantry — kills made on foot

54.2%

% of your lifetime kills made with infantry weapons. MAX counts as infantry here; vehicles, turrets and roadkills don’t. Shown from 100 kills (“—” until measured). Hover for your MAX and vehicle shares.

How it’s measured
  • Formula: foot kills ÷ all lifetime kills. Foot means kills with infantry weapons; MAX counts as infantry, vehicles, turrets and roadkills don’t.
  • Example: YuigahamaYui — 54.2%, about 54 of every 100 kills made on foot.

🚜 Vehicle: a 🚜 appears next to your name, like 🪦, when you mostly play in vehicles. It works like 🪳, and every line is taken from the data at each build: your infantry share is in the lowest quarter of all ranked players (below about 71.9%) and your Honu XP shows a lot of vehicle repairs or vehicle kills per minute (top quarter of players with 2+ hours of XP detail); or your infantry share is in the lowest 2% (below about 21.7%), even without XP detail. Hover it for the hint; the Infantry number itself does not change. On 🎥 LIVE the 🪖 turns into 🚜 below that infantry cut and fills: almost empty just under the cut, full (and shiny) at half the cut (about 36%) or less. Between the cut and the median (about 85.1%) the 🪖 stays empty; above the median it fills up to 100% infantry. Not the same as the 🚜 vehicle cut on ⚔️ iVi or the 🔀🚜 Diff reduction: this mark changes no number.

Marks and cuts on ⚔️ iVi

🚜 Vehicle + MAX cut on ⚔️ iVi

The first 10% of vehicle kills are free; every % above costs 1% of iVi. MAX kills work the same way, separately. The two add up.

  • 45% vehicle + 40% MAX → −35% −30% = −65%
  • 96% vehicle → −86%
  • Pure infantry → no cut
How it’s measured

Why: farming infantry from a tank, aircraft or MAX is easier than on foot. Hover the iVi value to see the cut. The site owner can exempt a player.

🪳⚔️ Zerg evening-out on ⚔️ iVi

Fighting inside a big crowd lifts the numbers a little beyond what you win on your own. We measured how much on real fights, take exactly that back, and give it to players who fight with less help.

How it’s measured
  • Measured, not guessed: 103,689 pairs of players who fought each other 30+ times. At the same ⚔️ iVi, each 🪳 step means about 0.08% fewer kills against the other player.
  • Both ways: 🪳 below 26.8 (the average crowd, where players without a 🪳 score fight) goes up, above it goes down. At iVi 1,000: 🪳 0 → +23, 🪳 50 → −20, 🪳 99 → −62.
  • Checked on players it never saw: fitted on half the players and tested on the other half, the 🪳 bias in their fights disappeared and fight predictions got slightly better.
  • Same weight in every tier; most players move by 1% or less. No 🪳 score or PlayStation → unchanged. Vehicles are not counted twice: the 🚜 cut already handles them. Away for 60+ days (no recent sessions) → unchanged until a refresh brings fresh data.

Research and numbers: docs/zerg-adjustment.md · discussion: issue #84. Hover ⚔️ iVi to see your before → after.

💩 Confirmed padders: −10% on ⚔️ iVi

Players confirmed farming kills on their own or friendly alt accounts get 💩 and lose 10% of iVi (kills on farm accounts are already left out). Hover 💩 for the reason. Think it’s wrong? Contact the site owner.

What 🛖 / ⛑️ means

🛖 next to a name: rarely dies, slow pace, low accuracy. That player's positive Diff is reduced, because it likely comes from safe positions.

How it’s measured

🛖 Spawn Room Warrior: rarely dies, plays at a slow pace and aims poorly, yet ⚔️ iVi beats IvI by a lot. That positive 🔀 Diff likely comes from safe positions, so it is reduced. The check gets stricter the higher the ⚔️ iVi and the longer the play time: newer and weaker players are judged gently, and players who die often are never touched.

⛑️ Squad support: the same pattern as 🛖 (rarely dies, a high ⚔️ iVi for the pace and aim), from a player who mostly heals, revives, resupplies or repairs their squad. Safe play, but outside the spawn room. The 🔀 Diff is lowered the same way.

📊 Census / Fisu / Honu style (the tracker numbers)

Lifetime numbers from Census, no adjustment. The same ones Honu and Fisu show.

KD
Kills ÷ deaths over your whole career, against everyone. Flatters players who farm weak opponents.
KPM
Kills per minute over your whole career.
own KPM
Kills per minute with your own weapons. Drives the iVi pace bonus or cut.
Acc %
Share of your shots that hit.
HSR %
Share of your kills that were headshots.
IvI
Census’s lifetime infantry score, with no opponent weighting. Compare it with ⚔️ iVi (see the example).

Example: 👉 YuigahamaYui

What trackers show: KD 1.71 · IvI 216, which looks below average.

What the site sees:

  1. Their 200 most-fought opponents kill at 0.96 per minute (a typical player’s opponents: ~0.35). Very hard opposition.
  2. ☠️ K/D against them: 826 ÷ 1027 = 0.80
  3. 🛡️ Resist: 0.80 × 0.96 = 0.773
  4. Rating: 600 × (1 + log₂(0.773 ÷ 0.6)) = 819
  5. Slow pace (own KPM 0.56, under 0.8): × 0.86 → ⚔️ iVi 708
  6. 🚜 vehicle kills 46%: −36% → ⚔️ iVi 455
  7. 🪳 Zerg 79, evened out to the average crowd (🪳 26.8) → ⚔️ iVi 416

Result: 416, better than 59% of tracked players (median 278). The public IvI said 216. Same sample: 🏃 Activity 0.45 · 🦁 Brave 1.09 · ⚡ Const 0.66.

🧘‍♂️ Pure iVi — your true solo ability

298

What your rating would be if every fight counted as if you fought alone. A high iVi on a zerg surfer says: you have it only because of crowd efforts. Pure iVi says: this is your true ability as a solo player.

How it’s measured
  • Formula: adjs above 0 → adjs × (1 − 0.00436 × 🪳); adjs at or below 0 → adjs − 0.00436 × 🪳 × 610.6. Each 🪳 point costs about 0.44% of the rating; 🪳 100 → −43.6%.
  • Measured, not guessed: fitted on 915 duel pairs (723 infantry-only). At the same adjs gap, each 🪳 point of gap meant 0.286 fewer kill-gap points against 0.656 per adjs point — 0.286 ÷ 0.656 ≈ 0.00436.
  • Example: ahorn, adjs 1225 at 🪳 87 → 1225 × (1 − 0.00436 × 87) ≈ 760.

Not a replacement for ⚔️ iVi, and not the 🪳⚔️ evening-out: that one centers on the average crowd (🪳 26.8) and lifts low-🪳 players up; Pure only ever discounts, never boosts, and has no freshness gate. PC only; no 🪳 score → “—”.

🔀 Diff — aim on paper vs real results

Your ⚔️ iVi percentile minus your public IvI (aim) percentile, from −99 to +99. Practice minus theory.

  • 🤥 Impostor (−33 or lower): great aim on paper, but real fights don’t convert it.
  • 😤 DieHard (+33 or higher): you win more than your aim predicts — positioning, fight choice, grit. Vehicle play can push it up too.
  • In between (−32…+32): balanced, aim and results roughly agree.

Neither side is better or worse. It shows a play style, not cheating.

🛖 next to a name: rarely dies, slow pace, low accuracy. That player's positive Diff is reduced, because it likely comes from safe positions.

Vehicle damp: above the typical vehicle share (about 15% of kills), a positive Diff counts only in proportion to your kills on foot (46% vehicle → ×0.63, 76% → ×0.28). If 🛖 also applies, only the stronger cut counts. Hover the Diff value to see it.

Newbie softening: a negative Diff is scaled by estimated play time (lifetime kills ÷ lifetime KPM). About 6 h or less (the newest 5% of players) → 0; then it rises smoothly to the full value at about 385 h (the median player). New accounts are still learning, so they are not called 🤥 Impostors yet. Positive Diff is untouched. Hover the Diff value to see it.

🐣 next to the name: a new player, under about 20 h played (the newest 14% of players), whatever the Diff. Up to that point about 29% of players read as 🤥 Impostors; after it the share drops. 🐣 also shows when a negative Diff was softened by 10 or more.
