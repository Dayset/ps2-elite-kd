The idea in one picture

Most ✨ columns read this line: your K/D against opponents from easy (left) to hard (right). It uses the ~200 players you fight most, not your whole career.

Why it matters: a player can show 3.96 lifetime K/D and only 1.15 against the people he really fights.

Why Honu and Fisu don’t show these

They look up one player. These columns also need the stats of your ~200 opponents, so this site keeps them all cached and refreshed. The 📊 Public columns are the same numbers the trackers show.

✨ Adjusted (the default columns)

⚔️ iVi — your overall rating

How good you are, once your opponents’ strength is counted.

  • Beating strong players counts more than beating weak ones.
  • Fast killing adds a bonus; slow, safe play costs a little.
  • Infantry only: vehicle and MAX kills above 10% lower it (🚜). Confirmed padders lose 10% (💩).
  • High: you beat strong players at a good pace.
  • Low: your wins come from weak players, or you play slowly. Below zero shows as 0.

🏃 Activity — winning fights, fast

Your ☠️ K/D × how fast you kill.

  • High: lots of fights won, quickly.
  • Low: you lose more than you win, or you kill slowly.

🦁 Brave — does it hold against strong players?

Your 🏃 Activity, checked against the fastest-killing enemies you meet.

  • High: you stay strong when the enemy gets strong.
  • Low with a high iVi: you pick easy fights and avoid even ones.

🛡️ Resist — K/D, weighted by enemy danger

Your ☠️ K/D × how fast your enemies kill. 1.0 K/D against killers is worth more than 1.0 against beginners. ⚔️ iVi is built from it.

  • High: you hold your own against fast killers.
  • Low: your K/D comes from slower enemies.

⚡ Const — do you hold up at the hard end?

Your K/D in the hard third ÷ your K/D in the easy third of the picture above (max 1). Shown as a percentile.

  • High (line stays flat): you do as well against hard opponents as easy ones.
  • Low (line drops): you farm easy opponents and struggle against hard ones.

Catch: someone equally weak everywhere also scores high, so Const is only a colour hint, never a rating.

☠️ K/D — against people you actually fight

Kills ÷ deaths against your ~200 most-fought players. Not your lifetime K/D. Every ✨ column starts from this.

  • High: you win most of those fights.
  • Low: you die more than you kill.

🪖 Infantry — kills made on foot

% of your lifetime kills made with infantry weapons. MAX counts as infantry here; vehicles, turrets and roadkills don’t. Shown from 100 kills (“—” until measured). Hover for your MAX and vehicle shares.

🚜 Vehicle + MAX cut on ⚔️ iVi

The first 10% of vehicle kills are free; every % above costs 1% of iVi. MAX kills work the same way, separately. The two add up.

  • 45% vehicle + 40% MAX → −35% −30% = −65%
  • 96% vehicle → −86%
  • Pure infantry → no cut

Why: farming infantry from a tank, aircraft or MAX is easier than on foot. Hover the iVi value to see the cut. The site owner can exempt a player.

💩 Confirmed padders: −10% on ⚔️ iVi

Players confirmed farming kills on their own or friendly alt accounts get 💩 and lose 10% of iVi (kills on farm accounts are already left out). Hover 💩 for the reason. Think it’s wrong? Contact the site owner.

📊 Public (the tracker numbers)

Lifetime numbers from Census, no adjustment. The same ones Honu and Fisu show.

KD
Kills ÷ deaths over your whole career, against everyone. Flatters players who farm weak opponents.
KPM
Kills per minute over your whole career.
own KPM
Kills per minute with your own weapons. Drives the iVi pace bonus or cut.
Acc %
Share of your shots that hit.
HSR %
Share of your kills that were headshots.
IvI
Census’s lifetime infantry score, with no opponent weighting. Compare it with ⚔️ iVi (see the example).

Example:

What trackers show: KD 1.71 · IvI 217, which looks below average.

What the site sees:

  1. Their 200 most-fought opponents kill at 0.96 per minute (a typical player’s opponents: ~0.35). Very hard opposition.
  2. ☠️ K/D against them: 826 ÷ 1025 = 0.81
  3. 🛡️ Resist: 0.81 × 0.96 = 0.776
  4. Rating: 600 × (1 + log₂(0.776 ÷ 0.6)) = 823
  5. Slow pace (own KPM 0.57, under 0.8): × 0.87 → ⚔️ iVi 713
  6. 🚜 vehicle kills 46%: −36% → ⚔️ iVi 457

Result: 457, better than 59% of tracked players (median 305). The public IvI said 217. Same sample: 🏃 Activity 0.46 · 🦁 Brave 1.10 · ⚡ Const 0.66.

Debug-only columns

Older or in-between numbers. Tick “show older debug stats” in the main page footer to see them. Not used to judge anyone.

⚙️ Mech%
A guess of your vehicle / mech share, projected from 🛡️ Resist. Not measured.
🎯🎈 ivi
⚔️ iVi before the pace bonus or cut, so slow play still looks good.
eKPM
How fast your opponents kill, on average. Higher = harder opposition.
own KPM
Same as the 📊 Public column.
📊 COI
🛡️ Resist ÷ 0.6. 1.0 = soft opposition; higher = harder.
📉 Slope
The angle of your line. Negative = your K/D drops against harder enemies. Feeds 🦁 Brave.
🎈 Inflation
Lifetime KD ÷ KD against enemies killing at 0.5+ per minute. Above 1 = easy fights inflate your KD. Lower is better.
