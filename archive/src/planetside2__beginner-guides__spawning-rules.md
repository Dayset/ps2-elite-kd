# Standard Spawns

![](http://cmdrcyrious.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/begin-spawn-locations.png)

**You can always spawn at:**

- Any **[Warpgate](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/bases/#Warpgate)** owned by your faction.
- Allied **[bases](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/bases/)** in **[regions](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/continents/#Regions)** close to your position.
- The closest **[Facility](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/bases/#Facilities)**, such as Tech Plant, AMP Station or Bio Lab.
- The closest base that has three or four **[Capture Points](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/bases/#Capture_Points)**.

You can choose a spawn location from the list on the upper left, or directly by clicking on a green circle on the main map.

**[Redeploy](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/spawning-rules/#Redeploying) hop:** when you want to spawn at a specific allied base, but it is too far from your position, you can keep redeploying to the closest base to your desired destination, until you get where you want, one base at a time. You can accelerate this process by pressing the redeploy key while on the loading screen.

**Avoid using the big green “Deploy” button or double clicking on the “Deployment” list.**

There is always a chance that spawn point will automatically change in the last second, and you’ll end up god knows where. To avoid this issue, deploy by double clicking on the spawn location on the map itself.

![](http://cmdrcyrious.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/map-spawn-1024x895.png)


## Respawn Timers

- Standard respawn timer is 15 seconds.
- Respawning at your last spawn location is 5 seconds faster.
- Respawning at [**Elysium Spawn Tube**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/spawning-rules/#Elysium_Spawn_Tube) takes 5 seconds longer. This penalty is compensated by “last spawn location” bonus.
- **[Redeploying](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/spawning-rules/#Redeploying)** reduces respawn timer by 10 seconds.

# Quick Deploy

![](http://cmdrcyrious.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/quick-deploy-1024x576.png)


**Quick Deploy** is available from the Death Screen, and it gives you an option to quickly respawn on your last respawn point.

If you last respawn point is destroyed or becomes unavailable for other reasons, another closest respawn point will be automatically selected.

You cannot use Quick Deploy if there are more than 60% of allies in the current **region**. You can still manually select the same respawn point from the main map, though.

This restriction will not apply if there are fewer than 12 allies in the region.


# Deployed Sunderers

![](http://cmdrcyrious.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/begin-deployed-sundy.png)


You can spawn at deployed **[Sunderers](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Sunderer)** within 1000m. They will also provide access to an **[Infantry Terminal](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/bases/#Infantry_Terminal)**.

# Reinforcements Needed

You can spawn on allied **bases** from any distance when they are under attack by superior enemy numbers, as long as the base is connected to your **Warpgate** via **[lattice](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/continents/#Lattice_System)** link.

Supposedly, Reinforcements Needed can activate only when there is at least one ally in the base’s **region**.

# Redeploying

Redeploying “kills” you without adding a death to your statistics. It is often done after a battle ends, so you can quickly get into combat elsewhere.

To redeploy, press the redeploy key (default “U”), or click the [**redeploy button on the main map**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/begin-redeploy.png). You will successfully redeploy if you do not deal or receive damage from enemies in the next 10 seconds. Your character will disappear, and you will be able to select a spawn location on the main map.

Redeploying cuts 10 seconds off your **[respawn timer](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/spawning-rules/#Respawn_Timers)**.

# Construction System

## Elysium Spawn Tube

![](http://cmdrcyrious.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/spawn-tube.jpg)


[**Construction System**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/construction-system-guide) allows players to build to build **[Elysium Spawn Tubes](http://cmdrcyrious.com/construction-system-guide/#Elysium_Spawn_Tube)** to provide a spawn location for allied players.

## Router

![Router](http://cmdrcyrious.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Router-300x169.png)


**Routers** are deployable spawn pads, which can be accessed from constructed **[Routing Spires](http://cmdrcyrious.com/construction-system-guide/#Routing_Spire)**.

# Join Combat

*Formerly “Instant Action”*

**Join Combat** can take you to any fight, where your faction’s population is within 20% of the enemy population. It will use nearest available spawn point, including [**Sunderers**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Sunderer) and base spawns.

Join Combat takes 10 seconds to activate and has a 2 minute cooldown.

Join Combat is known to be buggy, and will sometimes try to take you to fights that are already over, or completely hopeless. You can avoid this if you use Join Combat from the main map. Then Join Combat will show you the base where it wants to take you, and then you can decide whether you want to be there.

Prepare for a hard fight if you go to a fight where enemies have more than 60% population.

# Squad Spawning

If you play in a squad, you will have a few additional spawn options.

You can spawn directly into a **[Sunderer](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Sunderer)**, **[Galaxy](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Galaxy)** or a **[Valkyrie](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Valkyrie)** owned and occupied by a **[squad member](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/squads-and-platoons/)**. If the vehicle is in the enemy territory, or allied territory that’s being captured by the enemy, then you also have to be within 1000m of the vehicle.

You can spawn at an allied base from any distance, if majority of your squad are in that base’s region, and the base itself is connected to a Warpgate via lattice.

For the purposes of the “majority”, squad leader counts as two people.

## **[Logistics Specialist](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/implants/#Logistics_Specialist)**

Using this implant will allow your squadmembers to spawn directly into any vehicle piloted by you.
