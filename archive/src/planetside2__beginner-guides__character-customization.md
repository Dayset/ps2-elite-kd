Pictures above will show how you can customize the appearance of your character. On the “My Character” tab of the main menu, you can select a **title** and a **banner**, and a different **voice pack**.

- Titles are displayed before your character name, and they’re mostly tied to **[Battle Rank progression](http://planetside.wikia.com/wiki/Battle_Rank)**.
- Banners are visible on the **[Death Screen](http://cmdrcyrious.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/begin-death-screen.png)** whenever you kill someone.
- Voice Packs change your voice and can only be purchased with **[Daybreak Cash](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/character-progression-and-currencies/#Daybreak_Cash)**. Different factions use different voice packs.

It’s worth noting that cosmetic armor is completely transparent to bullets, and does not directly affect your character’s survivability in any way.

## Where to get more cosmetics

You can find a lot of cosmetics in the in-game store, though pretty much all of them will cost you some **[Daybreak Cash](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/character-progression-and-currencies/#Daybreak_Cash)**. Keep in mind that certain cosmetics unlock for your character of all **[factions](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/factions/)** at once, while others only for one certain faction. Check the items’ description to be sure.

There are many **[directives](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/character-progression-and-currencies/#Directives)** that award you with cosmetics:

- Fully completing the **[class](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/)** directives will award you with auraxium-coated composite armor for that class.
- Completing the **[Basic Training Directives](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/basic-training-directives/)** will grant you a Camo and a helmet for **[Light Assault](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/light-assault/)**.
- Completing the **[Social Directives](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/social-directives/)** will require you to purchase an Outfit Armor Decal with certs, and grant you an Outfit Vehicle Decal.
- Check your Directives tab to discover more.

You will get more decals whenever you reach a new **[Battle Rank](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/character-progression-and-currencies/#Battle_Rank)**.

You can get several decals by redeeming these codes in the in-game store:

- MLGREPORT
- PSUARMORDECAL
- PSUVEHICLEDECAL
- EQNVEHICLEDECAL

Decals are often **[awarded for winning](http://cmdrcyrious.com/known-alert-rewards/)** a Meltdown Alert.
