Who are we?

We are the New Model Army \[NMA\]. An NC outfit on Cobalt. Founded in autumn 2014 and one of the (if not) oldest still active German outfits on Cobalt. Our play style can best be described with “precap and hold”. This style of play is very infantry based and we don’t use vehicles that often. We play relaxed most of the time during the week. Except during our Ops that take place twice a week. During this time we play in a very competitive and organized way under an experienced lead.

Where: Cobalt

Approximate size: A total of more than 40 members of whom between 10 and 15 are active during peak time. Inactive members are regularly sorted out.

Contact Members: Ingame | Discord Athmosfear | \[NMA\] Athmosfear#8906 ChiChi25 | ChiChi#2680 DmC187 | DmC187#8071

Requirements to join: As we are a german speaking outfit, you should speak german as well. We pay more attention to chemistry between the members instead of pure skill – so be polite and a good company to others. Be able to work with Teamspeak3 and have a working headset/mic. We have a Code of Conduct and it is mandatory to follow that code. Our strength comes from teamplay instead of supporting some special unicorn players.
