**Advanced Specialization Program (ASP)** is the prestige system in PlanetSide 2. It is intended to provide veteran players with additional perks and unlocks that provide small gameplay bonuses and allow players to expand their loadouts with more options, including cross-class equipment.

# How to access ASP

![Advanced Specialization Program](http://cmdrcyrious.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/ASP-150x150.png)


Any character with **Battle Rank 100** or above can enroll into ASP using the “My Character” screen of the main menu.

Non-members have to pay **10 000 (ten thousand) Certification Points** to enroll, which is, incidentally, the maximum amount of certs a non-member character can have.

Enrollment is free of charge for **[premium members](http://cmdrcyrious.com/premium-membership/)**. You keep your ASP if cancel your membership afterwards.

Upon enrollment, your character’s Battle Rank will be reset to 1, but you will keep all your unlocks and certifications. You will also receive a new shoulder decal and title, and you will have a star icon near your Battle Rank number.

Your new maximum Battle Rank will be set at 100, which means your character will no longer be able to reach Battle Rank 120. If additional decals and titles are important to you, consider reaching BR120 before enrolling into ASP.

At Battle Ranks 1, 25, 50, 75 and 100 you will earn an **ASP token**, which you can spend on ASP unlocks.

# ASP Unlocks

Each ASP unlock costs 1 ASP token.

**Attention!** For now there is no way to refund or reset spent ASP tokens. Be careful with your choices!

## Universal Unlocks

*These unlocks affect all classes.* 

**Sidearm Primaries** – Allows use of **[sidearms](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/sidearms/)** in the primary weapon slot. *Currently lacks any actual combat-effective application. Possibly does not provide access to Auraxium Sidearms.*

**Universal Smoke Grenade Access** – Unlocks access to **[Smoke Grenades](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/light-assault/#Smoke_Grenade)** on all classes. *Could be useful if smoke wasn’t so [**inconsistent**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/smoke/#Problems). Try them on Light Assault before you spend an ASP point.*

**Universal Decoy Grenade Access** – Unlocks access to **[Decoy Grenades](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/infiltrator/#Decoy_Grenade)** on all classes. *Could potentially be useful in 1 – 12 / 1 – 12 battles. Try them on infiltrator before you spend an ASP point.*

**Light Ground Discount** – Reduces **[Flash](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Flash)** cost by 50% and **[Harasser](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Harasser)** cost by 20%.

**Ground Transport Discount** – Reduces **[ANT](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#ANT)** and **[Sunderer](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Sunderer)** cost by 20%.

**Combat Armor Discount** – Reduces **[Lightning](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Lightning)** and **[Main Battle Tank](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Main_Battle_Tanks)** cost by 20%.

**Light Air Discount** – Reduces **[ESF](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Empire-Specific_Fighters)** and **[Valkyrie](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Valkyrie)** cost by 20%.

**Heavy Air Discount** – Reduces **[Liberator](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Liberator)** and **[Galaxy](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/#Galaxy)** cost by 20%.

## Class-specific Unlocks

- These unlocks affect only one specific class.
- You can click on each class’ name for more info.
- Flash Grenade Access unlocks only class Flash Grenades, not the Quick-Det variant.
- Perks that provide access to additional weapon types do not allow to use Auraxium weapons (directive reward weapons).

### [Infiltrator](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/infiltrator/#Advanced_Specialization_Program)

**Sticky Grenade Access** – Unlocks access to **[Sticky Grenades](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/engineer/#Sticky_Grenade)** on the Infiltrator.

**Flash Grenade Access** – Unlocks access to **[Flash Grenades](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/light-assault/#Flash_Grenade)** on the Infiltrator.

### [Light Assault](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/light-assault/#Advanced_Specialization_Program)

**SMG Secondary** – Allows use of **[SMGs](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/smgs/)** in the secondary weapon slot.

**Concussion Grenade Access** – Unlocks access to **[Concussion Grenades](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/heavy-assault/#Concussion_Grenade)** on the Light Assault.

### [Combat Medic](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/combat-medic/#Advanced_Specialization_Program)

**Battle Rifle Secondary** – Allows use of **[Battle Rifles](http://cmdrcyrious.com/battle-rifles/)** in the secondary weapon slot.

**Carbine Access** – Unlocks access to **[Carbines](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/carbines/)** on the Combat Medic.

**Concussion Grenade Access** – Unlocks access to **[Concussion Grenades](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/heavy-assault/#Concussion_Grenade)** on the Combat Medic.

### [Engineer](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/engineer/#Advanced_Specialization_System)

**Shotgun Secondary** – Allows use of **[Shotguns](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/shotguns/)** in the secondary weapon slot.

**LMG Access** – Unlocks access to **[LMGs](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/lmgs/)** on the Engineer.

**Assault Rifle Access** – Unlocks access to **[Assault Rifles](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/assault-rifles/)** on the Engineer class.

**Anti-Vehicle Grenade Access** – Unlocks access to **[Anti-Vehicle Grenades](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/heavy-assault/#Anti-Vehicle_Grenade)** on the Engineer.

**EMP Grenade Access** – Unlocks access to **[EMP Grenades](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/infiltrator/#EMP_Grenade)** on the Engineer.

### [Heavy Assault](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/heavy-assault/#Advanced_Specialization_Program)

**Reserve Hardlight Barrier Access** – Allows use of **[Reserve Hardlight Barriers](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/engineer/#Reserve_Hardlight_Barrier)** in the Utility slot.

**Flash Grenade Access** – Unlocks access to **[Flash Grenades](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/light-assault/#Flash_Grenade)** on the Heavy Assault.

**Heavy Weapon Secondary** – Allows use of **[Heavy Weapons](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/heavy-weapons/)** in the secondary weapon slot.
