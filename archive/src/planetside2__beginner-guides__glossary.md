# [Weapons](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/) and [Mechanics](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/weapon-mechanics/)

- **ADS** – aim down sights. You hold your right mouse button to do that.
- **ADAD** – not an abbreviation. ADAD refers to A and D strafing buttons on the keyboard. “To ADAD” is to move erratically to left and right in the firefight to make yourself a harder target.
- **AoE** – area of effect. Refers to any effect applied over an area instead of to a single point. **[Frag Grenade](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/frag-grenades/)** does AOE damage, for example. Synonym for the splash damage.
- **AV, AI** – anti-vehicle and anti-infantry.
- **AP** – armor piercing (cannon) or anti personnel (mine).
- **AMR** – anti-materiel rifle, **[NS-AM7 Archer](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/sniper-rifles/#NS-AM7_Archer)**. Do not confuse with [**AMR-66**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/battle-rifles/#AMR-66), the TR Battle Rifle.
- **BASR** – **[bolt-action sniper rifle](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/sniper-rifles/#Bolt_ActionSniper_Rifles)**.
- **SASR** – **[semi-automatic sniper rifle](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/sniper-rifles/#Semi_Auto_Sniper_Rifles)** (or semi-automatic scout rifle). Infiltrator-only weapons. Don’t confuse with **[SAS-R](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/sniper-rifles/#SAS-R)**, short range NC BASR.
- [**CoF** – **Cone of Fire**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/weapon-mechanics/) – when you shoot, bullets are randomly distributed within the circle, called Cone of Fire.
- **DPM** – damage per magazine, equals to damage per shot, multiplied by magazine’s size. Also damage per minute.
- **DPS** – damage per second, equals to damage per shot, multiplied by rate of fire and divided by 60 (seconds in a minute)
- **ESRL** – empire-specific rocket launcher for heavy assault: Striker, Phoenix and Lancer.
- **ESSR** – empire-specific sniper rifle for infiltrator: TRAP-M1, Railjack and Phaseshift.
- (tank and Liberator cannons)
**HESH** – high-explosive squash head – anti infantry cannon with strong splash damage
**HE** – same as HESH.
**HEAT** – high explosive anti tank – jack of all trades
**AP** – armor piercing – anti vehicle cannon with almost no splash damage
- **LS / ALS** – Laser Sight / Advanced Laser Sight weapon attachments that increase hip fire accuracy.
- **OHK** – one-hit-kill – act of killing a player with a single high-damage shot.
- **TTK** – Time-to-Kill – how long will it take for a gun to kill a player.
- **Proxy, Proxie** – VS Proximity Mine
- **RoF** – rate of fire, in shots per minute.
- **RPM** – rounds per minute, same thing as RoF.
- [**UBGL**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/weapon-attachments/#Underbarrel_Grenade_Launcher_UBGL) – underbarrel grenade launcher – a weapon attachment, available to some of the weapons.

# [Vehicles](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/vehicles/)

- **AMS, S-AMS** – a deployed Sunderer, used as a spawn and resupply point for infantry.
- **ESF** – Empire-Specific Fighter, air superiority and close air support jet: Scythe, Mosquito and Reaver.
- **NAR** – Nanite Auto Repair System – a defense slot, that repairs vehicles and MAXes out of combat.
- **MBT** – Main Battle Tank – empire-specific two-seat tank: Prowler, Vanguard and Magrider.
- **Gal drop** – using a Galaxy (transport aircraft) to deploy infantry wherever needed.
- **Pull** (verb, “to pull”) – to spawn, to get. E.g., “somebody pull a Sunderer” means “somebody go to a vehicle terminal and spawn a Sunderer”.

# [Character](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/progressing-your-character) and Items

- **Adrenoweave** – a **HA loadout** that includes **[Adrenaline Shield](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/heavy-assault/#Adrenaline_Shield)** and **[Nanoweave Armor](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/suit-slots/#Nanoweave_Armor)**.
- [**XP**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/progressing-your-character/#Experience_Points) – Experience Points – granted for in-game activities. Each 250XP grants 1 cert.
- [**Cert**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/progressing-your-character/#Certification_Points) – Certification Point – an in-game currency, used to unlock weapons and upgrades.
- [**DB, DBC**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/progressing-your-character/#Daybreak_Cash) – Daybreak Cash – an in-game currency, used to purchase weapons and cosmetics.
- [**SC**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/progressing-your-character/#Daybreak_Cash) – Station Cash – old name for Daybreak Cash.
- [**Directives**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/progressing-your-character/#Directives) – PlanetSide 2’s version of achievements.
- [**Nanites**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/progressing-your-character/#Nanites) – personal PS2 resource, used to spawn vehicles and use consumables.
- [**LA**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/light-assault/) – Light Assult, infantry class.
- [**HA**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/heavy-assault/) – heavy assault, infantry class.
- **[NAC](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/infiltrator/#Ability_Cloaking)** – Nano Armor Cloaking, Infiltrator ability.
- **[NWA](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/suit-slots/#Nanoweave_Armor)** – Nanoweave Armor – a suit slot that provides bullet damage reduction on non-headshots.
- [**NMG**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/heavy-assault/#Nanite_Mesh_Generator) – Nanite Mesh Generator – Heavy Assault ability that creates a second layer of damage absorbing shields.
- [**MAX**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/max/) – a hybrid vehicle/infantry class with exosuit of armor and unique weapons.
- [**Loadout**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/loadout/) – a sum of equipment a character takes with himself into combat. There are loadouts for both infantry and vehicles.
- [**HSR**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/progressing-your-character/#Stats) – headshot rate – a player stat that refers to how many enemies a player kills with a headshot. Only the killing blow counts.
- [**SPM**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/progressing-your-character/#Stats) – score per minute – a player stat that refers to how much experience a player earns on average each minute. Affected by boosts and membership.
- [**Spot**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/map-and-minimap/#Spotting) – (verb, “to spot”) – you press \[Q\] by default to spot enemies, vehicles and explosives. Spotted enemies will have a triangle appearing over them, their name, and – if you are close enough – their health. Spotted enemies will also appear on the minimap. Spot lasts 10 seconds.
- **[Spawn Beacon](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/how-to-get-to-the-battle/#Squad_spawning), “Bacon”** – a deployable beacon that allows squad members to drop pod into action from large distances.
- **ES** – empire-specific. A piece of equipment that is available only to one faction, and features unique faction traits.
- **Ghostcapping** – act of capturing a base with no defenders. Usually involves standing around doing nothing while capture timer ticks.
- **Ops** – outfit ops, operations. Analogue of the “raid” term from MMORPG.
- **Zerg** – a huge group of players, e.g. “tank zerg” = “lots of tanks”.
- **Zerging** – having a massive numerical advantage over the enemy in a fight.
- **Zergfit** – an outfit that is often seen zerging.
- **Double Stack, Banana** – base building layouts.
- [**SCU**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/how-to-capture-a-base/#SCU) – a generator that powers the spawn room on some of the bases.
- **Forumside** – PlanetSide 2 [official forums](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/ps2/index.php).

# Memes

- Battlegoose, Beetlejuice – [**Betelgeuse 54-A**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/lmgs/#Betelgeuse_54-A)
- Pingu – [**NSX Tengu**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/smgs/#NSX_Tengu)
- Tiramisu – **[NSX Amaterasu](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/melee-weapons/#NSX_Amaterasu)**
- Damnyou – [**NSX Daimyo**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/sniper-rifles/#NSX_Daimyo)
- Keklone – [**AF-4 Cyclone**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/af-4-cyclone-highly-technical-weapon-guide/), often regarded as the best SMG and overpowered weapon.
- Trinity – refers to the trio of [**“close range” Bolt-Action Sniper Rifles**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/sniper-rifles/#Tier_1_8211_Close_Range) or [**close range LMGs**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/lmgs/#Close_Range_LMGs); both trios are widely regarded by competitive players as most effective weapons for “farming”.
- Meow-meow – Mjolnir, NC vehicle secondary weapon.
- Weaboo – wannabe japanese. Usually in reference to NSX weapon names.
- Wrelfare – equipment provided to all players for free after game’s release, such as quick reload shotguns, allegedly with the influence of Wrel – one of the game developers.
- Higby – one of the former PS2 developers. Often associated with NC having a ton of shotguns.
- PS2 profanity filter will replace obscene language with names of mostly former PS2 developers, such as Clegg and Higby.
