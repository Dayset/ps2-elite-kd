With the release of **MGR-L1 Promise** that heavily relies on Recoil Scaling Mechanics, there was a new breakthrough regarding said mechanics. And by “breakthrough” I mean that Wrel explained to me how they work. I’ve laid out that explanation with a few examples in the updated [**Weapon Mechanics Guide**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/weapon-mechanics/#Recoil_Scaling).

*It means so much to be able to talk to an actual living developer for one of my favorite games, can’t relay how thankful I am for Wrel’s explanation. Normally it takes hours upon hours of experiments just to get a theory on how it works behind the scenes, and being able to just get it on a silver platter from a dev is a huge relief.*
