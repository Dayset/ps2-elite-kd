# Equipment Availability

![](http://cmdrcyrious.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Equipment-3.jpg)


- All infantry classes use [**Frag Grenades**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/frag-grenades/) by default, but have access to additional grenade types, unique for each class.
- **[Med Kits and Restoration Kits](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/utility-items/med-kits-and-restoration-kits/)** are unlocked for all classes at the same time.
- [**Weapons**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/) and their **[attachments](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/weapons/weapon-attachments/)** are unlocked to all classes that can use them at the same time.
- **[Suit Slots](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/suit-slots/)**, **[C4](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/utility-items/c4/)** and other explosives need to be unlocked individually for each class.
- Most of the **[Implants](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/implants/)** are shared between classes, but some implants can only be used only by certain classes. Some implants are restricted from MAXes.

# Health and Shields

- Every class except for Infiltrators has 500 **shields** and 500 **health**.
- **Infiltrators** have 400 **shields** and 500 **health** by default, but they can increase shields to 500 by equipping Nano-Armor Cloaking ability.
- **Auxiliary Shield** utility item is available to every class for free and equipped by default. It passively increases **shields** by 50.

**Health** doesn’t regenerate by itself, unless you have a **[Regeneration Implant](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/implants/#Regeneration)** or a captured [**Biolab**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/bases/#Bio_Lab) on your current **[continent](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/beginner-guides/continents/)**.

**Shields** regenerate automatically:

- Shield regeneration starts in 6 seconds after you take damage. In 4 seconds for **Engineers**.
- It takes another 6 seconds to fully regenerate shields. This time can be reduced by 2-3 seconds by **[Advanced Shield Capacitor](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/equipment/suit-slots/#Advanced_Shield_Capacitor)** suit slot.

**MAX units** have 2000 base **health** and a slew of [**built-in resistances and vulnerabilities**](http://cmdrcyrious.com/planetside2/classes/max/#Built-in_Resistances_and_Vulnerabilities) to certain weapons. Importantly, MAXes take only 20% of damage from most bullet-type weapons, which means they have 10 000 effective health against bullets, meaning they take 2-3 magazines out of an average rifle to kill.
