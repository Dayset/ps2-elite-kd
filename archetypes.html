🧬 Player archetypes

Groups of players by how they actually play, from the stats this site already collects. The rules run in order and the first match wins, so every player lands in exactly one group. Click a row to see the group in detail — its medians and its top 10 players by ⚔️ iVi. Type a name to find your own archetype.

How the groups were made: a plain k-means over the same fields mostly just re-sorted players by skill, so these groups are a decision tree on the site's own numbers instead — reproducible from ranks.json alone. Medians are computed live from the current snapshot, so they move when the data refreshes. Hidden players are already left out of ranks.json. Archetypes are automated groupings from public stats, not verdicts about any player — see the disclaimer in the footer.
