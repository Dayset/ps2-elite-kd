01

Know your damage

How many bullets
to kill, by range

Every enemy infantry has 1,000 health (500 shield + 500 health). Your bullet does 167 damage up close, fading to 100 at long range. Headshots count double, leg shots count less (×0.9). That gives you three numbers worth memorising:

RangeDamageHeadChestLegsChest vs shielded Heavy
Close · 0–6 m1673679
Mid · 7–31 m167→12548912
Far · 32–46 m+125→1005101215

Perfect-accuracy times to kill: 3 headshots ≈ 0.18 s, 6 chest shots ≈ 0.46 s up close. In real fights you'll land maybe half your rounds — still, this is why the head matters so much on this gun.

Damage falloff — where your bullets get weaker

Full power to 6 m, then a steady slide to minimum at 46 m. The gold line shows Soft Point Ammunition, which stretches full power to 11 m. The 125 mark at 31 m is the last range where 8 chest shots still kill.

Rule of thumbInside 6 m you are at maximum power. Past 31 m every kill takes one or two extra bullets — close the distance or burst carefully instead of spraying.

0–8 mPoint blank

Hipfire — don't aim down sights

Aim: centre of the chest, from the hip. Keep moving.

Scoping in costs you time and drops your speed to 0.75× — at arm's length that gets you killed. SMGs have the tightest hipfire of any weapon class. Strafe side to side (A/D) while firing so you're hard to track.

8–20 mSweet spot

ADS, upper chest, confident bursts

Aim: neck / upper chest. Bursts of 5–8 rounds.

This is where the gun earns its keep: full 167 damage to 6 m, still 147 at 18 m, mild recoil, fast handling. Start your crosshair at the neck — the natural upward climb walks your shots into the head, and 3 head hits end the fight in 0.18 s. Strafe while scoped; at 0.75× you move faster aimed than a scoped Heavy, and a moving target still beats a still one.

20–46 mControlled

ADS, short bursts, pull straight down

Aim: chest. Bursts of 5 rounds, pause between them.

Damage is sliding from ~144 toward 100 and your cone of fire blooms if you hold the trigger. Fire a burst, release for a split second to let accuracy reset, fire again. The recoil climbs dead vertical (0° angle, no sideways drift), so pull your mouse straight down. Crouch or stand still when you can — both tighten your shots.

46 m +Plinking

Tap-fire only — or don't engage

Aim: chest. Taps of 3–5 rounds at exposed targets only.

At 100 damage you need 10 chest hits — a long, loud advertisement of your position. Take these shots at stationary or fleeing targets, medics reviving, and engineers on turrets. Otherwise, your better play is to close the distance behind cover. Knowing when not to shoot is a skill too.

03

Stances and firing modes

How you stand and
how you hold the trigger

Your accuracy is decided by two things before skill even enters it: what your body is doing (your stance sets the starting cone of fire) and what your finger is doing (holding the trigger grows that cone with every shot). The Tempest has no fire-mode switch — full-auto only — so "firing modes" are something you do with your finger, not a setting on the gun.

Hold – release – hold – release: the burst rhythm

Every shot widens your cone of fire ("bloom"). Releasing the trigger for a heartbeat resets it. Same 18 rounds, two very different outcomes:

The target pattern is bursts of about 5 rounds — that's the ideal. Bursts of 6–8 are fine while you're new (call it newbie tolerance). Past that, the bloom (0.06° per shot aimed, 0.06° from the hip) keeps growing with every shot, so the extra bullets mostly decorate the wall behind your enemy instead of hitting him. Short bursts also leave you rounds for the second enemy — remember, the magazine is 35 rounds, and a held trigger still eats it in 3.2 seconds.

TekLyte Reflex — 1x

No zoom. The target at 25 m is small but the view is wide open — you see the crate, the tower, and the next enemy coming. Fast to aim, nothing blocks your edges. Best inside ~25 m.

GD RefleXR — 2x

Same target, same 25 m — but he looks twice as tall, so placing shots on his chest and head gets much easier. The price: your view narrows to the round window, the crate and tower are gone, and close targets feel fast and huge. Best at 15–40 m.

That's the whole 30-cert decision from section 05 in one picture: 1x for awareness, 2x for precision. Most veterans carry both and swap per base — indoors 1x, open ground 2x.

Barrel — one slot, three trade-offs. "None" is a real choice.

No barrel attachment

Already yours0 certs

The Tempest's vertical recoil (0.3) is the highest of the five SMGs here, but it climbs dead straight up — learn the pull-down and plenty of veterans run this slot empty forever.

Good: your first weeks. Spend certs on your suit slots and the rest of your kit first.

Bad: nothing — there's no downside to an empty slot.

Compensator

Good100 certs

Cuts vertical recoil by 30%. Costs you: hipfire spread grows 15%, and enemies see you on the minimap from 35 m farther away when you fire. Your vertical recoil (0.3) is the highest of the five SMGs on this page, so the cut matters more here than anywhere else in the class.

Good: if you mostly aim down sights at mid-range and the upward climb bothers you.

Hybrid Suppressor

Situational100 certs

You vanish from the minimap when firing and your shots are quiet. In exchange, bullets fly 20% slower (360 → 288 m/s) — harder to lead moving targets.

Good: flanking behind enemy lines, rooftop and tree work, solo play behind the front.

Rail — pick ONE playstyle: aimed, hipfire, or ammo

Forward Grip

First buy100 certs

Cuts side-to-side recoil by 25%, keeping your bursts on a single target. Tiny cost: weapon swap is 0.15 s slower.

Good: the default for aimed, mid-range play — this gun's horizontal recoil (0.212–0.4) is the widest spread of the five, and the grip is the fix.

Bad: nothing meaningful. It only stops helping if you never aim down sights.

Laser Sight

Good — CQB build100 certs

Hipfire accuracy improves by a third (33% tighter cone). Replaces the Forward Grip — you can't have both.

Good: indoor point-holds and stairwell fights where you hipfire inside 8 m.

Bad: open bases — you give up aimed recoil control for a bonus you rarely use.

Darklight Flashlight

Tool, not a buff100 certs

A torch whose beam reveals cloaked infiltrators. Toggle it with the attachment key (X by default).

Good: hunting a stalker infiltrator camping your capture point — sweep corners with it.

Bad: as your everyday rail — it gives zero accuracy help, and the beam advertises your position.

Ammo — three options, each a trade-off

Soft Point Ammunition

Situational100 certs

Maximum damage range grows by 5 metres (6 → 11 m). Cost: bullets fly 10% slower (360 → 324 m/s).

Good: any fight inside ~20 m, where the extra 5 m of full damage is a whole extra bullet of kill power.

Bad: long-range tap-fire — the slower bullets are harder to lead, and past 46 m nothing changes anyway.

High Velocity Ammunition

Good — range build100 certs

Bullets fly 10% faster (360 → 396 m/s) and damage holds out 20 m further — minimum damage at 66 m instead of 46 m. Cost: full damage ends 2 m sooner (6 → 4 m).

Good: open-field fights past 20 m, where the extra reach is worth more than the metres of full damage you give up.

Bad: close-quarters brawling inside 10 m — the metres you lose are the range you fight in.

Disruptor Ammunition

Situational100 certs

Converts 20% of your bullet's damage into a disrupting pulse: every point converted drains 0.4% of the target's maximum shield health and ability energy.

Good: duelling Heavies and Medics — you drain their overshield and ability energy while you shoot them.

Bad: raw killing speed — a fifth of your damage stops hurting their health bar, so your time to kill gets longer.

What this gun can NOT mountShort Barrel, Heavy Barrel, Angled Forward Grip, Suppressor, Flash Suppressor, Extended Magazine — the last one is already built in — any underbarrel. High Velocity Ammunition IS available here, unlike on the base SMGs: the damage chart in section 01 has a second line only for Soft Point Ammunition, but High Velocity Ammunition stretches your minimum damage out to 66 m.

07

Kill efficiently: six habits

Technique beats
attachments

  1. Aim at the neck, harvest the head

    Headshots deal double damage: 3 kills up close versus 6 to the chest, in 0.18 s instead of 0.46 s. Don't aim at the head directly — aim at the neck and let the gun's natural upward climb carry shots two and three into the skull. It's the single biggest damage upgrade you own, and it's free.

  2. Burst, release, burst

    Every shot widens your cone of fire. Holding the trigger past ~8 rounds at range means later bullets fly around your target, not into it. Five rounds is the ideal burst; release the trigger for a heartbeat between bursts — your accuracy resets and your hits per magazine roughly double.

  3. Respect the first shot

    The first shot of every burst kicks 2.5× as hard as the rest — 0.75° of vertical jump before bloom even starts. In a burst, shot one is the wild one. Start your aim a touch low and let the first round climb into the chest, or tap the first shot and then hold.

  4. Reload early — the penalty is small, the habit is not

    Reloading with even 1 bullet left takes 2.23 s; an empty gun takes 3.6 s — well over a second more, so a dry magazine really can doom you. And 35 rounds vanish fast: after one kill and a few misses you're at half a mag. Duck behind cover and reload early anyway — the habit is what saves you on a gun that doesn't forgive.

  5. Pre-aim the doorway, then strafe

    Walk with your crosshair at head height, pointed where an enemy's head will appear — a doorway edge, a corner, stairs. Most beginners lose fights in the half-second spent dragging the crosshair onto target. While firing, tap A/D to strafe: at 0.75× aimed speed you're faster than almost anyone scoped in against you, and a moving target is dramatically harder to headshot back.

  6. Hipfire is a weapon, not a fallback

    Your standing hip cone is 1.0° — the tightest hipfire of any weapon class in the game — and the 0.14 m ring at 8 m fits inside his chest. Inside ~8 m, don't ADS at all: keep strafing and let the hipfire do the work. Past ~10 m the rings start leaking past him, and that's the exact moment to switch to the sight.

08

The honest weak spots

Know them,
play around them

Every gun has a price. Here's where the Tempest pays — and the habit that covers each hole.

1 · 35 rounds — generous, but the reload is the price

At 652 RPM a held trigger eats the whole magazine in 3.22 seconds, and 6 chest shots per kill means five kills per magazine at best — fewer with misses. The magazine is already extended — built in, with no equip-time penalty — so there is no Extended Magazine attachment to buy: 35 rounds is all you get.

Play around it: Burst discipline (habit 2) and early reloads (habit 4). Your 2.23 s short reload is slower than the Cyclone's 1.855 s — the price of the built-in magazine — so don't let the bigger magazine make you lazy about reloading.

2 · 652 RPM — the slowest fire rate of the five

Every missed shot costs you 92 ms — the longest refire of any SMG here — so a whiffed burst hurts more than it does on the 896 RPM Armistice.

Play around it: Make headshots count: 3 to kill up close in 0.18 s. Your per-bullet damage is the best in the class; trade on it, not on volume.

3 · The first shot kicks hardest

The first shot of every burst kicks 2.5× as hard as the rest — 0.75° of vertical jump before bloom even starts, the worst first-shot kick of any SMG on this page.

Play around it: Start your aim a touch low and let the first round climb into the chest — or tap the first shot, then hold. There is no attachment that fixes this one; it's technique.

4 · The sideways walk

Horizontal recoil of 0.212–0.4 with a tolerance of 0.9 is the widest sideways spread of the five guns here — the gun drifts left and right with no pattern to learn, and long bursts walk off the target's shoulders.

Play around it: The Forward Grip trims the sideways part by 25%, and short bursts (habit 2) never give the wander time to build.

5 · Long range is a pea-shooter

Past 46 m you're at 100 damage: 10 chest shots, 15 against a shielded Heavy. Soft Point Ammunition stretches your full damage to 11 m, and High Velocity Ammunition stretches the slide out to 66 m — but nothing lifts the 100-damage floor itself.

Play around it: Tap-fire at exposed targets only, and spend the rest of the time closing distance. You're an SMG — the front line, the doorway, the stairwell is your address.

09

Training: five VR drills

VR Training area
~15 minutes a session

Open the world map, travel to VR Training, and run these on the target dummies. Five minutes before a play session beats an hour of dying confused.

  • The Wall Test 2 minutes · recoil

    Aim at a wall 20 m away and empty a full magazine without touching the mouse. Watch the bullet pattern climb straight up — that's your gun's fingerprint (0° angle, no sideways drift). Repeat, this time pulling straight down, until the group stays fist-sized.

  • Neck-Line Walk 3 minutes · aim height

    Walk the dummy range keeping your crosshair glued to neck height the whole time, snapping to each new target without dipping to the floor or sky. Slow and clean first; speed comes on its own.

  • Burst Ladder 4 minutes · burst control

    At 20–30 m, kill dummies with bursts of 5, releasing the trigger fully between bursts. If a burst goes wide, shorten it. Goal: whole kills without a single wild round.

  • Circle Strafe 3 minutes · hipfire

    Pick a dummy, stand 5 m away, and circle it with A/D while hipfiring it down without stopping. This is your point-blank survival skill — the one that fires while your movement does the defending.

  • Reload Discipline 3 minutes · habit

    Fire exactly 15 rounds, step behind a pillar, reload, repeat. Train your hands to reload early and only in cover until it's automatic — in real fights you'll do it without thinking, which is the point.
