Weapon handling, advanced: where the numbers bite

A reading guide to the cone-of-fire numbers for every weapon with an aim-down-sights mode (386 weapons). It says which stance and firing mode pays for each class, and shows what attachments do to the numbers. Built from Census values, the same ones the weapon comparison page uses.

01 How to read the numbers

Three questions decide the stance for any weapon.

  • Does moving cost accuracy on ADS? Compare the ADS cone standing still and moving. Same number means strafing while aiming is free.
  • Does hip-fire hold at range? The body edge is the distance where the cone reaches the 0.3 m body half-width. Inside it, a shot lands on the body; beyond it, a shot can miss.
  • What does ADS cost? Census time to iron sights: 150 ms on 342 weapons, 200 ms on 15, 300 ms on 29.

Edges are geometry from the simulator's constants, not in-game tests. Use them to compare weapons, not as exact kill ranges.

04 Per-class rules

  • SMG (47): strafe and fire on ADS, 41 of 47 are free to move. Hip is the worst way to move (1.5° moving vs 1° standing).
  • Pistol (74): ADS while strafing for 59 of 74. Flare guns, Deep Freeze, CandyCannon and the NS-44L Blackhand family are the exceptions.
  • Carbine (42): only 4 of 42 keep the ADS cone while moving. Shoot from a standstill, strafe in short bursts.
  • Assault rifle, LMG, sniper, crossbow, hybrid (149): no strafe-free ADS; hip never reaches 30 m. Shoot from a standstill.
  • Scout rifle (32): 3 of 32 strafe free. Stand still for the aimed shot.
  • Heavy weapon (13): 6 of 13 strafe free; the Lasher X2 and NS-D Helios family are worse when moving.
  • Shotgun (29): strafe freely. The apparent hip accuracy at range comes from ignoring pellet spread; use damage falloff instead.

05 What is not modelled

  • In-game feel, recoil control and bullet travel time.
  • Heavy Magazine extra rounds, Compensator recoil on hip, and attachment effects not listed in the simulator table.
  • Shotgun pellet spread in the body-edge number.
  • Per-weapon sage-page sections: this guide is class-level; per-weapon pages are the next step.
