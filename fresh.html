PC room. 🪳 Zerg and 🧘 Pure use the same formulas as the Rankings table.

How the lists are made: fresh is a Census character age of at most 30 days at the snapshot (ranks.json updatedAt minus created), and the top 1% is the top 1% of the players with a 🧘 Pure value in the same room — both recomputed live from the current snapshot, so the cutoff and the names move when the data refreshes. The site's other "new" windows are wider (discovery queue 91 days, forensics 90 days); this page uses 30 days. Hidden players are already left out of ranks.json, and the fresh-account auto-hide (≤ 7 days old with adj ≥ 2200 and ekpm ≤ 0.729) hides fresh-exceptional accounts permanently — this page shows the survivors. Fresh accounts are automated groupings from public stats, not verdicts about any player — see the disclaimer in the footer.
