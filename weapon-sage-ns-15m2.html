NS-15M2 — the accurate one you buy

The steady, fast-handling LMG that any faction's Heavy Assault can carry — once you've bought it. Accurate bullets, gentle recoil, and the fastest aimed strafe of any LMG.

Read this first — this gun is NOT a starter. The NS-15M2 costs 1,000 certs (or 799 Daybreak Cash). No faction is handed it for free. If you play a Nanite Systems Operative, your free starter LMG is the XMG-100 — the NS-15M2 is a purchase you make later, because you want it, not a gun you start with.

The one-line verdict: the easiest LMG to keep on target — mild recoil that walks up and to the left, a quick 2.15 s reload, and you keep 75% of your move speed while aiming, where every faction LMG drops to 50%. Its price is honest: light 143-damage bullets and a slower kill time on paper than the faction starters. It rewards good aim and good movement, and punishes spray-and-pray. Fight between 10 and 40 metres and it will carry you.

01

First lesson: stop, aim, then fire

The mistake almost
every new player makes

Picture the scene, because you've probably lived it: you're sprinting across a courtyard, an enemy appears, and you do everything at once — hold the trigger down while you're still running, then press aim-down-sights (ADS) a split-second later, finger still down. The sight picture snaps in, it looks precise… and your bullets spray everywhere except the man in your crosshair. He kills you. It feels like the game cheated. It didn't — here's what actually happened.

What you did — the spray

Sprinting is the least accurate state in the game: your cone starts at 7.0° — fully bloomed, the widest it can ever be — and every hipfire shot widens it further (+0.1° per shot). Pressing ADS mid-hold changes the picture, not the cone.

✗ What you did — the spray

You started firing from a sprint, from the hip. Sprinting is the least accurate state in the game: your cone of fire starts at 7.0° — fully bloomed, the widest it can ever be. Every shot you fire from the hip widens it further still (+0.1° per shot).

Then you pressed ADS mid-hold — and nothing reset. Aiming down sights does not shrink a cone of fire that is already bloomed. The scope only gives you a precise picture; the bullets keep flying out of the wide cone you built while running and hipfiring. That's the whole mystery: accurate-looking aim, wildly inaccurate gun.

Two timers stack the deck further: your gun needs about 0.3 s to settle after a sprint, and raising the sight itself takes ~0.15 s. Trigger held through all of it means your first half-second of fire is pure confetti.

✓ The fix — three beats, in order

1 · Feet. Let go of sprint (and the trigger, if you're already holding it). Stop, or drop into a strafe — anything but a full sprint.

2 · Sight. Press ADS and let the sight come up. This takes a heartbeat — that's fine, your overshield (F) covers it.

3 · Trigger. Now fire. Starting from the aimed cone of 0.1°, your first shots land where the dot points, and you control the bloom from zero instead of inheriting a maxed-out cone.

Stop → aim → fire. If you take one habit from this entire page, take this one. You'll practise exactly this rhythm in the simulator in section 04.

The fix — three beats, in order

Three beats, always in this order. Stop first so the cone can shrink, aim second so the sight is up before any bullet leaves, fire third from a cone that starts at 0.1° instead of 7.0°. The whole sequence costs about half a second — your overshield buys it.

The hipfire exception — under ~8 m, skip all three beats Inside ~8 metres, don't ADS at all: hipfire and keep moving. At arm's length your hipfire ring is only 0.35 m wide (section 05), scoping in costs time and slows you to 75% speed, and stopping makes you an easy target. Here, stopping is optional — the movement is your defence. The disaster above is a mid-range problem: close enough that you reached for the sight, far enough that the spray missed.

Know your damage

How many bullets
to kill, by range

Every enemy infantry has 1,000 health (500 shield + 500 health). Your bullet does 143 damage up close, fading to 125 at long range. Headshots count double, leg shots count less (×0.9). Unlike most LMGs there are only two damage tiers to memorise — no middle step:

Perfect-accuracy times to kill: 4 headshots ≈ 0.29 s at any range, 7 chest shots ≈ 0.58 s up close and 8 chest shots ≈ 0.67 s past 10 m. In real fights you'll land maybe half your rounds — still, this is why the head matters so much on this gun: four head hits end it at any distance.

Damage falloff — where your bullets get weaker

Full power to 10 m, then a steady slide to minimum at 65 m. The gold line shows Soft Point Ammunition, which stretches full power to 15 m.

Rule of thumbInside 10 m you are at maximum power — 7 chest shots, 4 headshots. Past 10 m every kill takes one extra chest bullet (8 instead of 7), and the head stays at 4 all the way out. Close the distance or burst carefully instead of spraying.

Hipfire — don't aim down sights

Aim: centre of the chest, from the hip. Keep moving.

Scoping in costs you time and slows you down — at arm's length that gets you killed. The NS-15M2 shares the tightest standing hipfire of any LMG here (2.5° cone, tied with the XMG-100). Strafe side to side (A/D) while firing so you're hard to track, and let the overshield soak the return fire.

8–25 mSweet spot

ADS, upper chest, confident bursts

Aim: neck / upper chest. Bursts of 5 rounds.

This is where the gun earns its keep: full 143 damage, mild recoil, and you keep 75% of your move speed while aiming — strafe while you shoot, not just between bursts. Start your crosshair at the neck — the natural upward climb walks your shots into the head, and 4 head hits end the fight in under a third of a second.

25–50 mControlled

ADS, short bursts, pull down-right

Aim: chest. Bursts of 5 rounds, pause between them.

Damage is sliding toward 125 and your cone of fire blooms if you hold the trigger. Fire a burst, release for a split second to let accuracy reset, fire again. The recoil drifts up and to the left (−20°/−17°), so pull your mouse gently down and to the right. Crouch or stand still when you can — both tighten your shots.

50 m +Plinking

Tap-fire only — or don't engage

Aim: chest. Taps of 3–5 rounds at exposed targets only.

At 125 damage you need 8 chest hits — a long, loud advertisement of your position. Take these shots at stationary or fleeing targets, medics reviving, and engineers on turrets. Otherwise, your better play is to close the distance behind cover. Knowing when not to shoot is a skill too.

04

Stances and firing modes

How you stand and
how you hold the trigger

Your accuracy is decided by two things before skill even enters it: what your body is doing (your stance sets the starting cone of fire) and what your finger is doing (holding the trigger grows that cone with every shot). The NS-15M2 has no fire-mode switch — full-auto only — so "firing modes" are something you do with your finger, not a setting on the gun.

08

Kill efficiently: six habits

Technique beats
attachments

  1. Shield first, then shoot

    Your overshield (F key) is extra health that only helps if it's on before the bullets arrive. Hear footsteps or expect a corner? Shield up, then peek. A shielded Heavy needs 11 chest shots to drop up close instead of 7 — that gap wins duels.

  2. Aim at the neck, harvest the head

    Headshots deal double damage: 4 kills at any range versus 7–8 to the chest. Don't aim at the head directly — aim at the neck and let the gun's natural upward climb carry shots two and three into the skull. It's the single biggest damage upgrade you own, and it's free.

  3. Burst, release, burst

    Every shot widens your cone of fire. Holding the trigger past ~8 rounds at range means later bullets fly around your target, not into it. Five rounds is the ideal burst; release the trigger for a heartbeat between bursts — your accuracy resets and your hits per magazine roughly double.

  4. Never reload on empty

    Reloading with even 1 bullet left takes 2.15 s; an empty gun takes 2.5 s. That extra third of a second is smaller than on most LMGs, but it's still a third of a second standing defenceless. Count roughly: after two kills or ~35 rounds, duck behind cover and reload early. Treat the last 10 rounds as your emergency reserve, not your working magazine.

  5. Pre-aim the doorway, then strafe — while shooting

    Walk with your crosshair at head height, pointed where an enemy's head will appear — a doorway edge, a corner, stairs. Most beginners lose fights in the half-second spent dragging the crosshair onto target. And this gun lets you do what faction LMGs can't: strafe at 0.75× speed while aiming down sights and still hit things. A moving Heavy who is also landing shots is dramatically harder to headshot back.

  6. Pick fights inside 40 metres

    Your damage, accuracy and shield all shine at close-mid range. Sprint between cover, use buildings and rocks as stepping stones, and arrive at your range before opening fire. The player who chooses the range usually wins the duel — make it you.

09

The honest weak spots

Know them,
play around them

Every gun has a price. Here's where the NS-15M2 pays — and the habit that covers each hole.

1 · It loses the pure DPS race up close

143 damage at 625 RPM is 1,490 damage per second at best, falling to 1,302 at long range. The VS Orion and TR CARV both fire 750 RPM at the same 143 damage — if you and an enemy Heavy trade chest shots face-to-face, their bullets add up faster than yours. Your kill time is slower than every starter's, close and mid-range.

Play around it: don't trade fairly. Shield first, aim for the neck, and use your 0.75× aimed strafe — you're landing shots while moving faster than they can while aiming, and that movement breaks their aim more than their fire rate breaks you.

2 · Only 55 rounds — and no way to buy more

The CARV carries 100 and the XMG-100 carries 80. Your 55 vanish fast when suppressing a doorway, and there is no extended-magazine option on this gun at all — it simply doesn't exist.

Play around it: burst discipline (habit 3) and early reloads (habit 4). Your 2.15 s reload is the fastest here — reloading often is the design, not a failure.

3 · Long range is a pea-shooter

Past 65 m you're at 125 damage: 8 chest shots, 12 against a shielded Heavy, with bloom fighting you the whole way. Snipers and battle rifles own that ground.

Play around it: tap-fire at exposed targets only, and spend the rest of the time closing distance. You're a Heavy — the front line is your address.

4 · The recoil pulls up and to the left

Its recoil angle is −20°/−17° leftward, and the first shot kicks twice as hard as the rest (2.0×). In a long burst your aim walks off the target's left shoulder — you'll see this clearly in the VR drill below.

Play around it: pull your mouse down-right while firing, in the opposite direction of the climb. The Forward Grip already trims the sideways part by 25%, and the sideways walk is the smallest of any LMG here (0.2°).

5 · It costs 1,000 certs — and it's not stronger on paper

A thousand certs buys a lot of overshield ranks, suit slots and abilities. This gun does not out-damage the free starters; it out-handles them. If you buy it expecting a bigger hammer, you'll feel robbed.

Play around it: buy it for the handling — the 0.75× aimed strafe, the gentle recoil, the fast reload — and only after your shield and suit are in decent shape. And remember the swap time: 0.8 s to bring it out, so change weapons behind cover, never in the open.

10

Training: five VR drills

VR Training area
~15 minutes a session

Open the world map, travel to VR Training, and run these on the target dummies. Five minutes before a play session beats an hour of dying confused.

  • The Wall Test 2 minutes · recoil

    Aim at a wall 20 m away and empty a full magazine without touching the mouse. Watch the bullet pattern climb up-left — that's your gun's fingerprint. Repeat, this time pulling down-right, until the group stays fist-sized.

  • Neck-Line Walk 3 minutes · aim height

    Walk the dummy range keeping your crosshair glued to neck height the whole time, snapping to each new target without dipping to the floor or sky. Slow and clean first; speed comes on its own.

  • Burst Ladder 4 minutes · burst control

    At 30–40 m, kill dummies with bursts of 5, releasing the trigger fully between bursts. If a burst goes wide, shorten it. Goal: whole kills without a single wild round.

  • Circle Strafe 3 minutes · hipfire

    Pick a dummy, stand 5 m away, and circle it with A/D while hipfiring it down without stopping. This is your point-blank survival skill — the one that fires while your shield and movement do the defending.

  • Reload Discipline 3 minutes · habit

    Fire exactly 35 rounds, step behind a pillar, reload, repeat. Train your hands to reload early and only in cover until it's automatic — in real fights you'll do it without thinking, which is the point. Remember: there's no extended magazine on this gun, so the 55 you have are all you get.
