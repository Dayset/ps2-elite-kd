🚨 Rampage gate …

Burst sessions that are blatant hipfire headshot spraying. A match is flagged (impossible stats) and hidden from Rankings automatically: a pattern match, not proof.

ⓘ How it works
A 💥 burst session with ≥ 50 infantry kills, hipfire ≥ 90% and headshots ≥ 55% of them (Honu kill list, checked once per session on the regular refresh) = flagged and hidden from Rankings (🙈 hide list) with the reason “flagged: impossible stats — auto: rampage gate” and the evidence. Owner override: data/reviewed.json "burst": "cleared" un-hides the player and removes them from this list.

🚜 Vehicle + MAX cuts …
ⓘ How it works
⚔️ adjusted iVi ranks infantry only. Vehicle cut = vehicle / turret share of lifetime kills − 10%; MAX cut = MAX share − 10%. They add up, no cap (45% + 40% → −65%); the shares can’t pass 100%, so the total stays under 100%. No infantry data or under 100 kills = no cut. Stacks with the 💩 penalty (applied after it). A score at or below 0 gets a fixed drop (cut × 610.6). No public mark; the iVi hover lists each part.
