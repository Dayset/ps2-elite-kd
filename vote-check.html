🔐 How to check a vote

Anyone can check that oracle votes were recorded publicly and not changed later. No account needed.

What this proves

When you place a vote, the site makes a fingerprint of it: a long code built from the vote's details and a random secret number, the salt. When the alert settles, it makes a second fingerprint of the result.

Each day's fingerprints are combined into one seal. The seal is stamped into the Bitcoin blockchain. Bitcoin records cannot be changed afterwards, so the seal shows what the site had recorded that day. If a vote were changed later, its fingerprint would no longer fit the seal.

What it does not show

The public log holds only fingerprints and seals. It does not show who voted for what: no character names, amounts or outcomes. Only the voter, who has the salt, can check their own vote.

Step 1: check that a day was sealed (anyone)

  1. Open the list of sealed days: the sealed days list.
  2. Pick a day. Its entry shows the seal and whether it is stamped.
  3. Download that day's stamp file, named with the date (for example 2026-11-13.ots), from the same site under /votelog/.
  4. Check the stamp with the free OpenTimestamps client. A new stamp shows as pending until Bitcoin confirms it, usually within a few hours. After that the check gives the Bitcoin block and the date.

This proves the day's record existed on that date and has not changed since. It does not prove a particular vote is in it; that needs the voter's own check (Step 2).

Step 2: check your own vote (voter)

  1. Sign in to the oracle and open "All my votes". Each vote has its fingerprint (hash), its day and its salt. Keep the salt private.
  2. Copy the vote's details and its salt into the box at the bottom of this page. Nothing is sent anywhere: the check runs in your browser.
  3. "Matches" means your vote is inside that day's sealed record, unchanged since sealing.

If you want someone else to check your vote, show them your details and salt only. Do not post the salt publicly.

Check your own vote (stays in your browser)

Paste your vote record as JSON, in this shape: {"kind":"placed","values":[...details in the order shown on the site...],"salt":"...","hash":"...","path":[...],"root":"..."}. The first four fields come from your vote; path and root come from the public check for that hash.

✅ Matches: your vote is inside the sealed record, unchanged since sealing.

❌ Does not match. Check that every detail and the salt are copied exactly, or the vote has changed since it was sealed.
