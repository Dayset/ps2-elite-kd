🔐 How to check a vote

Anyone can check that oracle votes were recorded publicly and not changed later. No account, download or other app needed — tap a day below.

What this proves

When you place a vote, the site makes a fingerprint of it: a long code built from the vote's details and a random secret number, the salt. When the alert settles, it makes a second fingerprint of the result.

Each day's fingerprints are combined into one seal. The seal is stamped into the Bitcoin blockchain, and this page shows the block it landed in (Step 1). Bitcoin records cannot be changed afterwards, so the seal shows what the site had recorded that day. If a vote were changed later, its fingerprint would no longer fit the seal.

What it does not show

The public log holds only fingerprints and seals. It does not show who voted for what: no character names, amounts or outcomes. Only the voter, who has the salt, can check their own vote.

Step 1: check that a day was sealed (anyone)

Tap a sealed day below. This page asks the public log and shows the Bitcoin block the day's seal landed in — or that confirmation is still pending. No download, no other app.

Loading sealed days…

Advanced: you can still download a day's stamp file (named with the date, for example 2026-11-13.ots) from the public log and check it with the free OpenTimestamps client — the same seal, verified independently.

This proves the day's record existed on that date and has not changed since. It does not prove a particular vote is in it; that needs the voter's own check (Step 2).

Step 2: check your own vote (voter)

  1. Sign in to the oracle and open "All my votes". It lists each vote with its alert, amount and result.
  2. Showing each vote's fingerprint and secret number there — so you can check it in one tap — is still being built.
  3. The worked example below runs the same check in your browser, on sample votes.

If you want someone else to check your vote, show them your details and salt only. Do not post the salt publicly.

Check a vote record (stays in your browser)

If you have your vote record as JSON — fingerprint, salt, path and seal — paste it here. The check runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. The record is in this shape: {"kind":"placed","values":[...details in the order shown on the site...],"salt":"...","hash":"...","path":[...],"root":"..."}. The first fields come from your vote; path and root come from the public check for that hash.

✅ Matches: your vote is inside the sealed record, unchanged since sealing.

❌ Does not match. Check that every detail and the salt are copied exactly, or the vote has changed since it was sealed.
