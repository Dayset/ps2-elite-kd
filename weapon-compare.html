How this is calculated
  • Damage falls off in a straight line between each weapon's near range and far range.
  • Time to kill counts from the first shot. A reload is added after every full magazine.
  • Accuracy and headshots are worked out exactly (every possible outcome), not by random trials.
  • Not included: recoil, bloom, armor, attachments, first-shot delay and burst pauses, because Census does not provide them.
