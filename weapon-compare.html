Aiming uses each weapon's own aim-down-sights fire mode from Census: its cone per stance, bloom and recoil. Only one attachment per slot can be fitted (the wiki lists Heavy Barrel, Short Barrel, grips, lasers and Extended Magazine as rail attachments). Census has no attachment numbers: the effects (Compensator, Suppressor, grips, lasers, Heavy and Short Barrel, Soft Point, High Velocity) are typed in from the PlanetSide 2 wiki, patch 2023-02-08 or later. Bullet speed changes, Extended Magazine, slugs and flechettes are not modelled. The target is a head circle and a body box; the shot cone is filled evenly.

How this is calculated
  • Damage falls off in a straight line between each weapon's near range and far range.
  • Time to kill counts from the trigger pull. Weapons that wind up (NSX Yumi, NSX Kuwa) fire their first shot after the wind-up; burst weapons wait between bursts. A reload is added after every full magazine.
  • Accuracy and headshots are worked out exactly (every possible outcome), not by random trials.
  • The graphs above do not include recoil, bloom, armor or attachments. The simulator below them does include recoil, bloom and attachments, not armor.
  • The NSX Kuwa's wind-up is not in Census: 200 ms is typed in by hand.
