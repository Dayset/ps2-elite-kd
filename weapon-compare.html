Recoil simulator: best way to kill a target

Aiming uses each weapon's own aim-down-sights fire mode from Census: its cone per stance, bloom and recoil. Only one attachment per slot can be fitted. Census has no attachment numbers: the effects used (Compensator, Forward Grip, Angled Forward Grip, Comfort Grip penalty, Heavy Barrel, Short Barrel) are typed in by hand from the Izubrr repo and the Arsenal Update patch notes; any other attachment can be fitted but changes nothing here. The target is a head circle and a body box; the shot cone is filled evenly.

How this is calculated
  • Damage falls off in a straight line between each weapon's near range and far range.
  • Time to kill counts from the first shot. A reload is added after every full magazine.
  • Accuracy and headshots are worked out exactly (every possible outcome), not by random trials.
  • The graphs above do not include recoil, bloom, armor or attachments. The simulator below them does include recoil, bloom and attachments, not armor.
