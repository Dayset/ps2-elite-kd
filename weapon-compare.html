The reset, and the fine print
  • Resetting the cone: stop firing and recovery begins after a delay of one refire time (60,000 ÷ RPM milliseconds), then the cone shrinks at 20° per second down to your current stance's minimum. Standing still also clamps a bloomed cone instantly down to the still maximum CoF — famously small on the Naginata — but the 2018 Toolbox data doesn't store maximum CoF for any stance, so in the historical version that value is unknown unless you type it into the calculator yourself; the current Census data fills it in.
  • Direction changes: the free-shot count is your gun's clock. How often you flip direction is the enemy's clock — forum math on strafe speeds puts a useful flip at well under a second per direction, kept unpredictable. This page computes the gun side only.
  • NS-11C discrepancy, flagged honestly: Iridar's article text credits the NS-11C with 5 free ADS shots, but the v6h sheet's own numbers for it (still 0.1, moving 0.25, bloom 0.05) compute to 3. The table shows what the sheet computes; the article was likely written against older stats.
  • Rounding: counts are floored — a fractional shot doesn't exist. If the cone lands exactly on the moving minimum, that shot counts as free, matching Iridar's TRAC-5 example (4 shots: 0.1 + 4 × 0.05 = 0.3).

How this is calculated
  • Headshot rate is the share of shots aimed at the head; the rest are aimed at the chest. The graphs count every hit where it was aimed; in the simulator the cone and recoil can still push a shot off.
  • Attachments change every number on the page: the stat table (changed values are underlined), the graphs and the simulator.
  • Damage falls off in a straight line between each weapon's near range and far range.
  • Time to kill counts from the trigger pull. Weapons that wind up (NSX Yumi, NSX Kuwa) fire their first shot after the wind-up; burst weapons wait between bursts. A reload is added after every full magazine.
  • Accuracy and headshots are worked out exactly (every possible outcome), not by random trials.
  • The graphs leave out recoil, bloom and armor. The simulator adds recoil and bloom, not armor.
  • Simulator: each weapon's own aim-down-sights or hip fire mode from Census (cone per stance, bloom, recoil). The target is a head circle, a body box and legs (0.9× damage); the cone is filled evenly. Bullet speed is not modelled.
  • Census has no attachment numbers: most effects are typed in from the PlanetSide 2 wiki (patch 2023-02-08 or later); magazine and ammo changes are read from each attachment's own description. One attachment per slot.
  • The NSX Kuwa's wind-up is not in Census: 200 ms is typed in by hand.
  • With the experimental FPS limit on, every weapon's fire rate is cut by 0.0132·RPM/FPS (fitted from measurements), so damage per second, time to kill and the recoil simulator all slow down together. Reloads and wind-up are unchanged.
  • Tier lists rank every weapon by effective DPS while strafing: burst DPS at the page range, times the moving cone's hit chance, with the FPS limit applied when it is on.
