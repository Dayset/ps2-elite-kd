🔒 Privacy

The short version. Full details: PRIVACY.md · data sources: DATA_SOURCES.md

👤 Players

Characters shown here come from public game data (Daybreak Census API and Honu): name and id, outfit, stats, weapons, opponents and login times. Nothing else: no real names, emails or locations. We don't know who plays a character.

Why: community stats, rankings and fair-play checks. Legal basis: legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Ratings and marks are automated estimates, not facts or accusations. Think a mark on your character is wrong? Open an issue for a human review.

🚫 Remove your character

Don't want your character here? Remove it yourself below. We delete our copy, stop fetching it, take it out of Rankings and other players' opponent lists, and show “removed at the player's request” instead.

Only the player can do this: you prove the character is yours by logging out once in the game. Can't sign in? Delete the character in the game, or ask Daybreak support to delete your account: once Daybreak no longer has it, our copy is deleted automatically within about two days. We only keep what Daybreak publishes; we can't remove it from Daybreak or other sites.

🖥️ Visitors

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers.
  • Your browser keeps your settings and cached stats on your device only. “Clear local data” in the footer deletes them.
  • Live stats come straight from Daybreak's servers to your browser, so Daybreak sees your IP address.
  • GitHub (hosting) and Cloudflare (our two small servers) see IP addresses. We store them only as a scrambled code for rate limits, for about an hour.

🗳️ Oracle sign-in

Signing in stores your character, a sign-in token, your BonusCheques (no cash value) and your votes. Sign-in attempts are deleted after a day. Ask in an issue to delete your account.

⏳ How long we keep it

  • Player stats: while the character exists, or until you ask for removal.
  • PlayStation sessions: 45 days.
  • Oracle map history: 30 to 90 days.
  • Older copies stay in the public Git history of the project.

✉️ Your rights and contact

Access, correction, objection, deletion: open an issue; we answer within a month. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Security problems: SECURITY.md.
