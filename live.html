♿ What about vehicles?

⚔️ iVi measures infantry skill only. If more than 10% of your lifetime kills come from vehicles (seats, turrets), the share above 10% is subtracted from adjusted iVi; MAX kills above 10% are subtracted the same way, separately. The two add up, with no cap: e.g. 96% vehicle kills → −86%; 45% vehicle + 40% MAX → −65%. Pure infantry is untouched. Hover a player’s iVi value to see the cut, and the 🪖 Infantry column for the shares (details).
