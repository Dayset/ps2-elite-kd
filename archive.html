📚 Archive

The CMDRCyrious / Iridar PlanetSide 2 knowledge archive — preserved here because the original is vanishing.

What this is

A preservation archive of the PlanetSide 2 knowledge base that CMDRCyrious hosted at cmdrcyrious.com — above all, Iridar's work: the Weapon Mechanics guide, the Construction System guide, the “Highly Technical” analysis method, the Weapon Analysis Toolbox, and the videos. CMDRCyrious spent years hosting and preserving Iridar's guides, tools, and videos; this archive takes over that role. We are the knowledge database now.

Why it exists

The data is vanishing while we watch. On 2026-11-09, when this archive was made, the live site still answered HTTP 200 for every page — but 64 of its 159 pages served no article body at all in their static HTML: navigation shell, empty content. Of those 64, 51 were recovered from the Wayback Machine and 1 (Weapon Mechanics) was already held as a 2023 snapshot; 12 pages have no Wayback snapshot anywhere and their text is lost. That is the state of the record as of 2026-11-09, and the reason this archive exists.

Who this is for

Someone looking for this information 10 years from now. Every entry is written to stand on its own: original author and date, source URL, snapshot or archive date, what the item is, its lineage (who built on whom), and its currency status — still working, outdated, dead, or lost — with the verification date where one is known. No entry assumes you were here when it was made.

Lineage — who built on whom

  1. Iridar (2016–2018) wrote the Weapon Mechanics guide, the Construction System guide, the “Highly Technical” analysis method, the Weapon Analysis Toolbox spreadsheet, and the angular-size research. He also made the videos.
  2. CMDRCyrious hosted and preserved Iridar's work on cmdrcyrious.com — guides, Toolbox, videos, the Weapon Simulator — and added his own highly-technical weapon guides, mechanics explainers, and calculators on top of it.
  3. Halospud wrote the Infantry Guide that CMDRCyrious linked from his infantry-guides page; it credits Iridar and teaches the movement layer Iridar's numbers don't cover.
  4. EmperorDorf (2022–2023) built the Weapon Surgeon simulator on Iridar's Toolbox stats — his stats page is Iridar's Toolbox data. His projectile-radius finding (0.03 m) then corrected Iridar's angular-size research, closing the exact in-game vs model gap Iridar himself had found and published in 2017.
  5. This archive (2026-11-09) takes over CMDRCyrious's preservation role, because the live site is already losing pages.

The angular-size correction runs through the whole collection: Iridar's angular-size research predicted headshot rates from target angular size. In 2017 he tested it himself in VR and found 57% headshots observed against 44% predicted. In that same thread, u/BoatsFriends proposed the projectile-size hypothesis; Iridar confirmed a large bullet hitbox. In 2023 Dorf quantified it: a 0.03 m projectile radius reproduces Iridar's numbers exactly. Anything here built on angular size is dead for that reason, and is marked so. The replacement is Dorf's method: simulate with a 0.03 m projectile radius.

How to read the currency statuses

✅ Verified working (2026)Checked in the live game by Dayset in 2026. The one hard currency fact in this archive.
🕰️ Method timeless, data datedThe math and mechanics still hold; the weapon numbers are from the stated year.
⚠️ Largely outdatedThe game systems described have changed since; the exceptions that still work are named.
💀 Dead (kept for history)The research or method was disproven — by its own author, or by later measurement. Kept because the correction is part of the story.
❌ LostThe text is unrecoverable: the live copy is empty and no Wayback snapshot exists. What remains is noted honestly.
⏳ PendingNot yet captured when this archive was made. The URL and the reason are recorded so a later reader can try again.

The Iridar core

These seven items are the core of the archive. Everything else supports them.

PlanetSide 2 Weapon Mechanics

Iridar · 2016-04-25 · 🕰️ Method timeless, data dated

The complete guide to how PlanetSide 2 weapons work: cone of fire (including the stance-change rules behind the ideal-ADAD rhythm), recoil, bloom and recovery, damage calculation, attachments. The single most important document in this archive.

Construction System Guide

Iridar · 2018 · ⚠️ Largely outdated

The full construction guide: modules, the core, placement, and the preview-placement trick. Modules and the core are gone since 2018 — except the preview-placement trick, which still works in 2026. Also saved as a Wayback snapshot and a mirror copy.

“Highly Technical: How to analyze a weapon”

CMDRCyrious (Iridar's method) · 2017-07-03 · 🕰️ Method timeless, data dated

The method paper: which weapon stats to look at to properly analyze a weapon — CoF bloom three ways, CoF recovery, initial accuracy, ADS speed as a survival trait. Worked comparison: NSX Tanto vs NS-11C.

Iridar's Weapon Analysis Toolbox (spreadsheet, v6h)

Iridar · 2018-08-19 · 🕰️ Method timeless, data dated

The Toolbox itself: 10 sheets, per-weapon damage, CoF per stance, bloom, recoil statistics, Monte Carlo horizontal deviation. 194 weapons. Data is 2018 (old Nanoweave, pre-rebalance); the method is timeless.

“Weapon Analysis Toolbox Spotlight” (video)

Iridar · 2017 · ⚠️ Mixed

A walkthrough of the Toolbox: Gauss SAW vs Nanoweave TTK, HVA effects, the Recoil page, Cyclone vs Armistice. The CoF analysis in it is dead by the author's own warning (angular size); the rest is a 2017-era feature tour. No video file stored; transcript unavailable.

Angular Size testing post (Reddit)

u/Iridar51 · 2017-09-10 · 💀 Dead (kept for history)

The post where Iridar published his own debunk: the Eridani VR test gave 57% headshots observed against 44% predicted. The hinge of the whole collection — the moment the old model died and the radius model was born. Saved by hand as a browser snapshot on 2026-11-09, post and 23 comments.

“[MECHANIC] Preview placement before construction” (video)

Iridar · 2018 · ✅ Verified working (2026)

The video demonstration of the construction preview-placement trick. The trick it shows — Ctrl+F11 to hide the weapon + E to place without starting construction — was verified still working in the live game in 2026. No video file stored; the video has no speech.

Supporting material: Halospud's Infantry Guide — Part 2: Shooting Mechanics and 1v1 Basics and Part 4: Movement Tricks — teaches the movement layer the numbers don't cover.

The Toolbox spreadsheet

Iridar's Weapon Analysis Toolbox, v6h (2018-08-19), as CMDRCyrious hosted it. Dorf's Weapon Surgeon stats page is this data, and the Ideal ADAD Calculator computes its still-shot counts from this sheet. Download it here — the original upload link is one of the things that can vanish next.

How it was made

Inventoried from the site's sitemaps (170 URLs; 159 PlanetSide 2 pages kept, 11 non-PS2 skipped). Every kept page's raw HTML was downloaded from the live site; 64 of 159 had empty bodies, 51 were recovered from the Wayback Machine (trust the CDX API, not the availability API — it falsely reported 45 pages as having no snapshot), and 33 Wayback downloads arrived gzip-encoded and had to be gunzipped in place. HTML was converted to markdown with trafilatura; the two Halospud Google Docs came from the Docs export endpoint. Nothing here was republished or uploaded anywhere else; this is a local archive, now hosted on this site. The full method notes and inventory live in the repo at archive/src/INDEX.md.
